# Polyra learner app (prototype)

Scope: the learner's daily loop on a phone: home, word card, exercise, answer feedback, session end,
settings, skill sheet. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and task: Persian-speaking adults learning French or German; tap the jelly to start, work
through a 15-minute mixed session, see honest progress. Constraints: RTL Persian UI that can switch to
LTR, both themes designed, reduced motion respected, no emoji, no gamification devices.

Memorable moment: the shape-shifting jelly with the "polyra" wordmark that squashes under the finger
and starts the session, and the station drop that lands at session end.

Unresolved: owner has not yet reacted to this build. Background tint, font pair and gender colors are
proposals (see `DECISIONS.md`).

## Direction contract

THESIS: One playful, touchable object on a calm page. The jelly carries the brand and the fun; every
other surface stays quiet and legible so the app never reads as childish.

OWN-WORLD: The owner's six colors only: raspberry #D81E5B for action, deep raspberry #A8134A for press
and wrong answers, light raspberry #F0527F as its highlight, blush #FDE7EF for tinted surfaces, aubergine
#2B1020 for ink by day and ground by night, teal #1FB5A8 for correct answers and the streak freeze.
Day ground is a lighter blush (#FFF6F9). Fraunces with the SOFT axis for the wordmark, levels and the
target language; Vazirmatn for Persian. Generous radii (20 to 22), soft shadows tinted toward aubergine,
duotone skill icons.

STORY: The learner sees today's session and level, touches the jelly, meets each word in a large
article-colored headword with sound, answers, and ends the session with a station drop that falls in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (language, level chip, streak, freeze, settings). Subscription line under it.
The jelly fills the hero with "polyra" on it; under it the session summary and a first-time hint, then
the short-session link. Four skill cards in one row (icon, name, level, progress, "⋯"). Links list below.

FORM: Owner-pinned brief: palette and the jelly wordmark Start come from the owner's own message, so
gradient wordmark text is intended. Motion lives in the jelly only; elsewhere springs are short.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
